Dragonet: your dragon companion

Dragonet is a small wild dragon that visits every map in 4 Heroes. It is shy, it is always hungry, and if you earn its trust it becomes a companion that flies beside you, spits acid at your enemies and, once fully grown, carries you through the sky. This guide covers everything from the first careful meeting to riding a level 15 dragon.

Meeting the wild Dragonet

The wild Dragonet only shows up when things are calm. If enemies are within roughly 120 meters of you it stays away, and if a fight comes close while it is sitting nearby it flies off. The first visit usually comes within a minute of entering a map, and after that it returns every one to two minutes. Sometimes it simply flies across the sky. More often it lands on the ground you are standing on and sits there for about 45 seconds.

A thought bubble above its head shows the food it wants. Dragonet is a picky eater: it only asks for things that grow on My Farm, namely carrots, tomatoes, corn, strawberries, pumpkins, apples, pears and cherries. If the item is in your farm bag, you can feed it. If not, harvest some on your farm and try again on its next visit.

How to sneak up and feed it

The bar under the wild Dragonet measures how nervous it is. Anything sudden within about 33 meters fills the bar: running, jumping (a single jump scares it a lot), attacking or firing a shot close to it. When the bar is full, Dragonet panics and flies away. Standing still slowly calms it down again.

  1. Stop as soon as it lands and let the fear bar drop.
  2. Walk towards it a little at a time. Stop whenever the bar starts to climb.
  3. When you are right next to it, stand completely still for a moment. Dragonet takes the food from you and eats it.

Every meal adds one point of friendship, which is saved to your account, and gives you 30 coins. Each point also makes Dragonet a little braver, so every meal makes the next one easier. Ten points of friendship, ten meals in total, and Dragonet decides to stay with you for good. You receive a 300 coin bonus on that day.

Your companion in battle

A tamed Dragonet follows you to every map, from The World to the bonus maps. It flies just behind your head, and when an enemy comes within range it joins the fight in two ways:

  • Acid spit. A round green drop that flies straight at the nearest enemy, about every 1.5 seconds.
  • Dash strike. Every few seconds it rushes at an enemy that is close and bites it. The bite hits much harder than the acid (1.8 times the acid damage), but Dragonet loses 5 health of its own each time.

Everything Dragonet hits counts as your attack, so the coins and experience from the kill are yours. When no enemies are around and you stand still for a couple of seconds, it lands on your head or on the ground next to you to rest.

Health, rest and healing

Dragonet has its own health bar, which you can see in the Dragonet menu on the main screen. Enemies can hurt it, and every dash strike costs a little health. When its health runs out, it does not die. It flies home to your farm, exhausted, and rests there. A resting Dragonet does not fight. It comes back into battle once it has recovered 40% of its health.

There are two ways to heal it:

  • Feed it from the Dragonet menu. Crops and fruits from your farm bag restore health equal to about half of their market price, from 6 up to 45 health per item. A blue apple restores 30. Each meal also gives Dragonet experience.
  • Buy the pet house for your farm in the Shop (My Farm decor, 1,500 coins). While Dragonet is resting or staying at home, it heals 1 health every 20 seconds by itself, even when you are offline.

If you want to play without your companion for a while, choose Leave on my farm to rest in the Dragonet menu. It sleeps in its house and heals there until you take it with you again.

Levels and growth

Dragonet levels up separately from your heroes, from level 1 to level 15. It earns experience by fighting (1 point for each acid spit, 2 for each dash strike) and by eating (4 points per meal). Each level needs 40 points times the current level, so level 2 needs 40 points, level 3 needs 80, and the last step from 14 to 15 needs 560. Reaching level 15 takes 4,200 experience in total.

Each level adds 1.5 damage to its attacks. Dragonet also grows bigger at levels 5 and 10, and at level 15 it becomes a full-size dragon three times its original size, large enough to carry a hero.

Upgrades

The Dragonet menu has three upgrades that you buy with coins. Each one has five steps, and a new step unlocks every three Dragonet levels: the first at level 3, the second at level 6 and so on up to the fifth at level 15.

UpgradeEach step
Acid power+4 damage to the acid spit, and to the dash strike through its multiplier
Fluff armor+20 maximum health (base health is 70)
Quick wingsSpits about 0.14 s sooner and dashes about 0.38 s sooner

The five steps of each upgrade cost 300, 600, 1,000, 1,500 and 2,200 coins, so maxing one upgrade costs 5,600 coins and all three cost 16,800. If you play mostly alone, Fluff armor keeps Dragonet in the fight longer. In a full team, Acid power adds the most damage.

Riding the dragon

At level 15 Dragonet is big enough to ride. In The World and on My Farm, wait until it lands on the ground next to you (stand still with no enemies around), then press E next to it. Steer with the arrow keys or the joystick. The dragon flies twice as fast as you can run, which makes it the quickest way to reach a far island or come back to a village to sell your loot.

  • One flight covers about 300 meters. When the dragon gets tired it glides back down, and it needs 15 seconds of rest before it can fly again.
  • Press jump to get off at any time.
  • You cannot ride during a boss fight, and the dragon will not land near enemies.

Quick tips

  • Keep a few crops in your farm bag before you go exploring, so you can feed the wild Dragonet whatever it asks for.
  • Do not fight near a wild Dragonet. Clear the area first, then wait for it to land.
  • Buy the pet house early. It turns every break from the game into free healing.
  • Feed Dragonet even when it is healthy enough: meals give experience, and a level 15 dragon is worth the effort.
