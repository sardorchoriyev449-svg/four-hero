Heroes and levels

Each hero plays differently and levels up separately. This guide explains what each one is good at and what it unlocks, so you can choose the hero that suits you and your team.

How levels work

Experience is earned per hero. Up to level 15 each level needs about twice as much experience as the one before it, so the early levels go quickly. After that the growth becomes gentler, and the level cap is 100. From level 20 onward, every tenth level adds a further bonus: +5 stamina at levels 20, 40, 60 and so on, and +5 damage at levels 30, 50, 70 and so on.

Knight

The Knight is the best hero for beginners and the natural front line of a team. Sword swings cost little stamina, and holding Shift raises a shield that blocks most incoming damage. In a team the Knight should stand between the boss and the ranged heroes. At level 3 the shotgun adds a close-range burst, and at level 10 the fly boots let you reach ledges or escape an attack that covers the ground.

Archer

The Archer attacks from a safe distance and is at her best against bosses that can only be hurt in one spot, such as a head on a long stalk. Shift makes her invisible, which is useful for slipping past enemies or getting out of trouble. Stay at range and keep moving: she has none of the Knight's protection. Fire arrows at level 4 are a big jump in damage.

Mage

The Mage deals powerful magic damage, but every spell drains stamina quickly, so careful timing matters more than with any other hero. Shift summons a tornado. Ice balls deal less damage than fire but freeze enemies, which can buy your team a few valuable seconds. From level 10 the Mage becomes the team's healer, and that alone makes the hero worth bringing to a hard boss.

Samurai

The Samurai is the fastest hero, with sharp katana strikes and a dash on Shift that crosses gaps and cuts through enemies. It rewards players who like to attack and move away again, rather than stand and trade blows. The katana spirit at level 3 gives it a ranged slash for targets out of reach, and the double jump at level 10 makes the islands of The World far easier to cross.

Hunter

The Hunter is a premium fifth hero built around preparation. A long knife covers the middle distance, and Shift throws a sticky mine that aims itself at the nearest enemy. Later levels add a blowpipe for long-range shots and poison that keeps hurting a target after the hit. The Hunter is strong against enemies that chase you, because you can lead them across your mines and traps.

Building a team

A team of four does not need one of each hero, but a mix helps. A Knight holds a boss's attention, an Archer or Hunter deals steady damage from range, and a Mage keeps everyone alive with healing from level 10. Because each hero has a separate level, it is worth keeping a second hero levelled so that you can fill whatever role your friends are missing.
