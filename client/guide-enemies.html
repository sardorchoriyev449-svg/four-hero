Enemy guide

Bosses get the spotlight, but most of your time in The World is spent fighting ordinary enemies. Every location has its own crew, and each enemy has a habit you can learn and use against it. This guide goes through them location by location, with screenshots taken in the game. For the bosses, see the boss guide.

How enemies work

Enemies live in groups: camps by a campfire, small patrols on bridges and islands, and single guards. They notice you when you come close, chase you while they can still see you and walk back home if you get far enough away. Enemies get tougher from one location to the next. A Knife Thief in the Elf Kingdom is a fair fight for a new hero, while a Guard Bot in Skyland hits as hard as a small boss. The world guide lists the recommended level for each location.

Three things are worth knowing about almost every enemy:

  • Shields only cover the front. Enemies with a shield sometimes raise it when you hit or shoot them. A raised shield stops attacks from the front, but a hit from behind or from the side still lands. Jump over them, or let a teammate attack from the other side.
  • Warnings come first. The big attacks (leaps, lasers, roots from the ground) are announced: a crouch, a red aiming line, a crack in the ground, a "!" mark. Learn the warning and you can step out of the way every time.
  • Some things are not what they seem. Mimics pretend to be a chest, a snowy hut, a tree stump or a rock until you walk up to them.

Elf Kingdom

Thieves have invaded the kingdom where every adventure begins. They wear hoods, so you only see their white eyes, and they are the gentlest enemies in the game. Use them to learn how shields and ranged attacks work.

Knife Thief

Quick and nimble. It runs up and stabs with a knife, and sometimes raises a small shield when you attack it. Back off a step when it raises the shield, then strike as soon as it lowers it to attack.

Thief Archer

Shoots arrows from a distance and prefers the highest platform of the camp. Its arrows fly straight, so a jump or a step to the side avoids them. Close the gap quickly: up close it has no answer to a sword.

Thief Brute

A heavy thief with armor and a big shield. It is slow, but its blows hurt and knock you back, and it blocks more often than the others. Circle behind it or let your ranged heroes hit its back while the Knight holds its attention.

Evil Sorcerer Elf

A dark elf who floats in the air and throws bolts of black magic that fly in a straight line. Melee heroes struggle to reach it, so this is a job for the Archer, the Mage, a jump attack or your Dragonet.

Mimic

Next to a stack of crates you may find one chest too many. Sometimes that chest is a Mimic: it jumps up with a snap of its teeth when you come close. It is tougher than a thief and pays more coins and experience.

The Forest

Hunters camp among the trees, and some of them have brought a pet lizard. Forest enemies are a step up from thieves and work well together, so fight them one at a time if you can.

Knife Hunter

Fights with two knives and slashes quickly and often. Each cut is weak, but they add up fast. Do not trade blows with it for long. Hit, step back, hit again.

Spear Hunter

Throws spears in a high arc from far away. A spear lands where you were standing, so keep moving and the spears land behind you. Spear Hunters often stand on the far side of a camp. Run straight at them and they are easy to beat.

Shield Hunter

Armored and patient, with a sword and a shield it raises often. Like the Thief Brute, it can be beaten from behind or by a team that attacks from both sides.

Clawed Lizard

A giant lizard that stands twice as tall as a hero when it rears up. It is fast, strikes with its claws from further away than you expect and has plenty of health. Ranged heroes should keep their distance. A Knight with a raised shield can hold it in place.

The Snow Base

The Snow Base has fewer enemies per camp, but the ice makes every fight harder: the ground is slippery and blizzards push you around. Watch your footing near edges.

Snow Kid

A small kid in a snowsuit who throws snowballs in a high arc. Snowballs are easy to dodge one at a time, but several Snow Kids together cover a lot of ground. Rush them: they have little health.

Ice Ram

A big icy beast, larger than a hero. Up close it punches and throws you back. At medium range it crouches for half a second and then leaps onto you, and the landing shakes the ground around it. When you see the crouch, run toward it or away from it. Do not stay where you are standing.

Hut Mimic

Some of the snowy huts at the Snow Base are alive. Walk close and the hut stands up on two big arms, twice as tall as a hero. It hits hard, but it is slow to turn. A hut standing alone, away from a village, is worth a careful look before you walk past.

The Swamp

The misty Swamp is dark and full of mud that slows you down. Its people do not like visitors.

Swamp Savage

A wild fighter who rushes at you and hits hard. It is tougher than the hunters of the Forest, so do not let two of them pin you between them. Fight with your back to open ground.

Root Hood

A figure in a black hood with glowing yellow eyes. It never comes close: it keeps a distance of about 26 meters and casts a spell under your feet. First the ground cracks, then roots burst out of it and throw you into the air. You have almost a second between the crack and the roots, enough to step aside. Chase it down: it is much weaker up close.

Stump Mimic

An old tree stump that is not a stump at all. Like the other mimics, it waits until you are very close and then attacks. Stumps on their own, away from the trees, are the ones to watch.

The Underworld

Deep below the surface, miners work the glowing ore. It is dark down here: their helmet lamps light up the way ahead of them, and you will often see the light before you see the miner.

Miner

Fights with a pickaxe. A Miner is slower than a thief, but each swing hits very hard. Wait for the swing, step back, then strike while it recovers.

Miner Shooter

Carries a drum gun that fires chunks of ore. It only shoots at heroes it can see on screen, so it does not hit you from the darkness. It still has a long reach, so close in quickly or take cover behind rock.

Mimic Rock

A black rock that grows legs when you come close. It jumps onto you first: a "!" mark appears on the ground where it will land, and you have two seconds to get away. Then it fires three fireballs in an arc from the crater on its head. Keep moving between the jumps and attack while it is reloading.

Skyland

High above the Elf Kingdom and the Forest float the islands of Skyland, guarded by robots. There is usually just one robot per island, but each one is twice as tall as a hero, has a lot of health and hits hard. Skyland robots are also smart: they remember the islands and stepping stones you used and follow your trail, jumping precisely from one to the next. If you get more than about 70 meters away or stay out of sight for a few seconds, they give up and walk home.

Drone

A round metal head on two long wire legs, with a red lens that fires a laser. First a dashed red line shows where the laser will go, and the direction is fixed at that moment. Then the beam fires. If you dodge, the Drone quickly aims again with a shorter warning, so expect a second shot. It does not fire through island rock, and if you corner it, it jumps over your head to the other side.

Guard Bot

A copper robot with a blue head, a shield in one hand and a holographic laser sword in the other. It often blocks attacks from the front with its shield, but it cannot block while it is swinging or lunging, and it is always open from behind. When it is on the same island as you and has room, it lunges forward. When it cannot reach you, it raises its shield and waits below you.

General tips

  • Pull enemies one at a time. Step into a camp's view, then back off so that only the nearest enemy follows you.
  • Deal with ranged enemies first. Archers, sorcerers and Root Hoods do the most damage when you ignore them.
  • Use the terrain. Many enemies cannot follow you onto a high ledge, and shooters cannot hit you through rock.
  • Rest at checkpoints. Fighting through a Skyland island at half health is how most heroes fall.
