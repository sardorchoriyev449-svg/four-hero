The World

The World is the main map of 4 Heroes: a huge chain of floating islands that you and up to three friends explore at your own pace. The layout is generated for your world and your progress is saved, so you can come back to it whenever you like.

How the map is built

The surface has three locations in a row. The Elf Kingdom is in the middle, the Snow Base lies to the west and the Forest lies to the east. The Swamp is underneath them, and below it is the Underworld, which is closed for now and will arrive in a later update.

Each location is made of several routes rather than one road. Small stepping stones high in the sky form the fast route, the ground route passes through villages and enemy camps, and the lower route leads to caves and secret rooms. If a jump looks impossible, there is usually another way around.

Checkpoints, villages and portals

Flags mark checkpoints along the way, and touching one saves your position. When you die you return to the last checkpoint you touched, so it pays to go out of your way for them before a dangerous stretch.

Each location has two villages with a shop and people to talk to. Shops sell the same goods at the same prices everywhere, so you never have to walk back to buy a cure. Each location also has a portal, usually in a village, that takes you to any location you have already reached and saves a very long walk.

For 600 coins, the Treasure Map adds a world map that you open with M. It shows bosses, checkpoints and portals. Click any place on it and an arrow points the way, counting down the distance in metres. It also shows where your friends are, which is the easiest way to meet up.

Recommended levels are advice rather than a locked door. You may enter any location early, but enemies there hit harder, and your level is what reduces that damage.

Elf Kingdom

Thieves have taken over the roads around the elves' villages. Knife thieves rush you, archers shoot from a distance and big brutes carry shields, while evil sorcerer elves cast spells from behind them. Watch out for treasure chests, because some of them are mimics that bite. The elves also need help: a mother elf is looking for her two children, and the Fat Elf's old meadow is the place to look. A cave nearby is the home of the Robot Snake.

The Forest

Tribal villages with thatched huts and totems stand among the trees, and hunters camp on the islands between them. The lizards here are the real danger: they can pull you in with a long tongue, and they sometimes turn invisible and sneak up on you. If one disappears, keep moving and listen for it. The Forest also hides a farm with a very large carrot. Think carefully before you touch it.

The Snow Base

The ice under your feet is slippery, so plan your stops early, especially near an edge. Blizzards roll in and make it harder to see. Snow kids throw icy snowballs that chill you and slow you down, while ice rams walk on one giant arm and leap on top of you. Any snow hut standing alone deserves suspicion: it may grow crab legs and giant ice claws when you get close. You can also pick up snow from the piles and throw it yourself.

The Swamp

The Swamp is the hardest open location, a dark and misty place below the surface. Thick mud slows you down, so avoid falling into it during a fight, and fog makes distant enemies harder to spot. Swamp savages bite at close range, root hoods attack with dark magic, and an innocent-looking tree stump may turn out to be a mimic. Stilt houses and a boatman mark the villages, which are welcome safe spots after a long stretch of fighting.

Camp scouts and quests

Look for a scout with a yellow exclamation mark at the edge of an enemy camp. Scouts in every location hand out random jobs that send you to real places on the map: defeat a certain number of one enemy, clear a number of enemies anywhere in the location, catch fish at the location's lake, or gather something from its woods: yellow apples in the Apple Orchard of the Elf Kingdom, wild berries in the Berry Wood of the Forest, ice crystals among the Frost Pines of the Snow Base, or lilies in the Lily Marsh of the Swamp. The quest panel on the left shows your progress and points you towards the place.

You can carry one quest at a time. When it is done, report to any scout in the same location to collect coins and experience, and they will offer you a new job. Harder locations pay more.

Lakes and fishing

Every location has its own quiet lake, marked on the world map. Bring a fishing rod, which any village shop sells for 100 coins, and press E by the water to cast. When a fish bites, a bar appears: stop the moving marker on green for the full price or on yellow for a little less. On red the fish gets away. Fish go to your inventory, where you can sell them, and rods break after 20 casts, just like on your farm.

Tips for exploring

  • Break every crate you pass. Coins from crates add up quickly early on.
  • Clear camps from a distance if your hero can. Fighting on a narrow island makes it easy to be knocked off.
  • Secret rooms and caves often hold the best rewards, so explore the lower routes.
  • In a team, agree on a location first. Portals and the Treasure Map make it easy to regroup.

When you are ready for the biggest fights in each location, read the boss guide.
