My Farm

My Farm is the quiet side of 4 Heroes: your own land where crops keep growing while you are offline. It is also one of the steadiest ways to earn coins for your heroes. Only the room's host can plant and harvest, while friends can visit and watch.

Crops

Each field has six beds. Choose a seed, plant it and come back when it is ripe. New seeds unlock with your farm level, which grows as you harvest, fish and complete orders.

Times are at normal speed, before watering, weather and upgrades. Carrots are the best way to start: they are nearly free and ready in half a minute. Slower crops earn more per harvest and suit times when you will be away. Flowers sell for little, but the Swamp Mage loves them.

Watering a bed makes it grow 1.5 times as fast. Fertilizer from the shop increases the harvest and gives a 15% chance of a golden crop, and the seasons and the rain also change the growing speed. Sell your harvest from the inventory. Two products are on Today's Offer each day and sell for double, so check them before you sell everything.

More land

Your first field is free. The next three cost 250 coins each, the fifth costs 400 coins at farm level 3 and the sixth costs 600 coins at farm level 6. From farm level 2 you can buy a rake for 800 coins and dig up to 12 extra beds in the open meadow. The inventory holds 80 items plus 40 for each farm level, so a higher level means less time spent making trips.

Fruit trees

Trees cost more than seeds, but they keep giving fruit without being replanted, so they are a good long-term choice. You start with three orchard spots and get one more at farm levels 3, 6 and 9.

Fishing

At the far end of the farm there is a river. Build a dock for 200 coins, then buy a rod for 100 coins or a net for 300 coins. With the rod, wait for a bite and stop the moving line on green for the full price. Yellow pays less, and on red the fish gets away. The net needs no skill and catches two to four fish at once, but it can only be used every 30 seconds. Both tools break after 20 uses.

There are perch, carp, pike and catfish. Larger fish are worth more, and pike and catfish can only be caught with the rod. A very rare goldfish can also bite, and it has a very narrow green zone. If you catch one, it is worth far more than anything else in the river.

Elf helpers

From farm level 3 you can hire up to two elves, for 1,500 and then 3,000 coins. An elf harvests ripe crops on its own. If you have placed a chest in your farmhouse, the harvest goes there. It also replants each empty bed with the crop that grew there before, using your coins, and waters it. Elves become faster as they gain experience, and between jobs they have a life of their own: they read at home, play cards if you own a card table, and swim if you own a pool. Keep some coins and some chest space free, or they will have to stop working.

Your farmhouse

Press E at the door to go inside. Open your inventory and press the Furniture button to see everything for sale, from a simple chair to a piano and an aquarium, then press E at a free spot in the house to place it. You can buy the same item several times, up to the number of matching spots in the house, except for the chest, which is limited to one. If you change your mind, you can sell any item back for 90% of its price.

The chest is worth buying first. It stores 300 extra items, and it is where your elves deliver their harvest.

Daily farm routine

  1. Harvest everything and check Today's Offer before selling.
  2. Complete any orders on the board by the farmhouse: elves pay much more than the market.
  3. Replant, with fast crops if you will be back soon and slow ones if you won't.
  4. Cast the net at the river, and spend the coins on your next upgrade.

If you stay away for a long time, mushroom creatures may appear and start eating your crops. Clear them out as soon as you get back.
