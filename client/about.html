4 Heroes - a co-op pixel action game

4 Heroes is a free multiplayer action game that runs right in your browser, on a computer or a phone. Create a room, invite up to three friends with a room code and fight your way through a story of robot snakes, stone gorillas, giant flowers and a legendary kraken. Every map is a different challenge - some are races, some are puzzles and some are huge boss fights that only a team can win.

How to play

  1. Create an account - pick a nickname and a password. Your coins, hero levels and unlocked maps are saved.
  2. Create a room or join a friend's room with its room code. A room holds up to 4 players.
  3. Press Ready. The host chooses the map and presses Start.
  4. Clear the map together. When everyone who is still alive reaches the goal (or the boss falls), the next map opens for the whole team.

The heroes

Knight

A melee sword fighter. His shield blocks attacks, so he is the one who stands in front of the team.

Archer

Quick arrows from a safe distance. At level 4 her arrows catch fire and hit harder.

Mage

Powerful fireballs and ice orbs, but every spell drains stamina fast. At level 10 the Mage learns to heal the whole team.

Samurai

The fastest hero: sharp katana strikes and a speed dash through enemies.

Every hero has its own level. Clearing maps and defeating enemies gives experience (XP); new levels unlock stronger attacks, a second weapon and special skills. Coins buy skins, weapon skins and costumes.

Maps

Season 1

  • The start - a flat, open field: the simplest place for your first battle.
  • Just run - a giant robot snake breaks through the wall. Dodge mines, pick the right path and land on the checkpoint.
  • Mini game - a sky-blue elf throws mega apples from a giant tree. Catch 12 in your basket.
  • Why are we here? - the city market. Talk to the fishmonger elf - he knows something...
  • Just run 2 or end boss - the robot snake smashes into the market. Shoot it until it falls.
  • Bad dogs - evil robot dogs wait by the giant tree in the forest.
  • Walking stones - swaying stones over a bottomless pit. Each stone collapses one second after you land on it.
  • Gorila Rock boss - a giant stone gorilla roars, shoves and smashes platforms into the ceiling.
  • Right on time - you fell into the lair of a hungry Fat Elf... and you are next on the menu.
  • UnderWorld - glowing nature, tree houses, strange creatures and a man-eating flower.

Season 2

  • Giant Flower boss - the road is blocked by thorns and a giant flower. Only its head can be hurt.
  • Mountain Lift - get everyone on a huge platform and ride up the mountain while red bots attack.
  • Farm - a quiet farm with strange vegetable beds. Just one carrot... what could go wrong?
  • Legendary Kraken boss - a misty river and a kraken sleeping in the deep. Hit its red eye.
  • Yellow Door - someone is screaming for help behind the yellow door...

Bonus: Arena

Endless training. Bots keep coming and every fifth kill calls a random boss. Every kill gives coins and XP to the player who made it, and every defeated boss rewards the whole team. The Arena opens when the host's hero reaches level 5.

Playing together

  • Rooms can be public or private. Private rooms are joined only with the room code.
  • The lobby has a text chat and an optional voice chat (the microphone is used only after you press the voice button).
  • Players who fall become ghosts until the team clears the map - nobody is left behind.

FAQ

Is the game free?

Yes. 4 Heroes is free to play. You can optionally watch an ad to get bonus coins.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The game runs in any modern browser. On a phone you can add it to the home screen to play full screen.
