4 Heroes - a co-op pixel action game

4 Heroes is a free multiplayer action game that runs right in your browser, on a computer or a phone. Create a room, invite up to three friends with a room code and explore The World: a huge map of floating islands with villages, enemy camps, hidden rooms and giant bosses. When you want a break, go home and run your own farm.

How to play

  1. Create an account - pick a nickname and a password. Your coins, hero levels and progress are saved.
  2. Create a room or join a friend's room with its room code. A room holds up to 4 players.
  3. Press Start. Rooms open in The World, and the host can choose a bonus map instead.
  4. Explore together. Fight your way across the islands, touch checkpoints and take on the bosses of each location.

New to the game? Our beginner's guide covers your first hour.

The heroes

Knight

A melee sword fighter. His shield blocks attacks, so he is the one who stands in front of the team.

Archer

Quick arrows from a safe distance. At level 4 her arrows catch fire and hit harder.

Mage

Powerful fireballs and ice orbs, but every spell drains stamina fast. At level 10 the Mage learns to heal the whole team.

Samurai

The fastest hero: sharp katana strikes and a speed dash through enemies.

Every hero has its own level. Defeating enemies and bosses gives experience (XP); new levels unlock stronger attacks, new weapons and special skills. Coins buy upgrades, skins, weapon skins and costumes. See Heroes and levels for everything each hero unlocks.

Free to play

4 Heroes is free. All the maps, all four heroes and online co-op are free to play, and nothing in the game is sold for real money. Upgrades, skins and costumes are bought with coins that you earn by playing.

The World

The World is the main map of the game, and it is made of several connected locations. The Elf Kingdom is where you begin, the Forest lies to the east, the Snow Base to the west and the Swamp below them. Each location has its own enemies, two villages with a shop, a portal, a recommended hero level and bosses to find, some of them secret. The deepest location is the dark Underworld, below the Swamp. Read more in The World guide and the boss guide.

Bonus maps

My Farm

Your own land, where crops keep growing while you are offline. Plant crops and fruit trees, fish in the river, hire elf helpers and furnish your farmhouse. Only the room's host can work the farm, and friends can visit. See the My Farm guide for prices and growing times.

No More Friends

An arena where friends become rivals: every hero fights for themselves against the other players, and the first to 5 victories wins. A defeated hero comes back after 3 seconds. The Knight's shield blocks most of the damage.

Playing together

  • Rooms can be public or private. Private rooms are joined only with the room code.
  • The lobby has a text chat and an optional voice chat (the microphone is used only after you press the voice button).
  • In The World a defeated hero comes back at the last checkpoint, and the world map shows where your friends are.
