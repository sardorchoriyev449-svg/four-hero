Boss guide

Each location in The World has its own bosses, and each one is really a puzzle: learn what it does and where it can be hurt, and the fight becomes much fairer. This guide contains spoilers for every boss, including the secret ones.

How boss fights work

  • Health grows with the team. A boss's health is multiplied by the number of players fighting it, up to four, so two players face twice the health. Bosses become tougher with each location. Main bosses start at about 1,900 HP in the Elf Kingdom and reach about 3,100 HP in the Underworld for one player. Secret and side bosses have even more.
  • Bosses get angry. Most become faster and more dangerous when their health falls below half.
  • They need rest too. After a few attacks many bosses stop to catch their breath. This is your best moment to deal damage.
  • Walking into a boss is usually safe. Simply touching most bosses will not cost you health, although some push you away. Their attacks are what hurt you, and most of those are announced by a warning.
  • Do not run too far. If you run away from a fight, the boss recovers all its health.

Elf Kingdom

Fat Elf

Found in an old meadow, the Fat Elf talks to you first and then attacks. Up close it breathes green fire, and from a distance it spits three blobs of acid into the air. The acid is the real problem, because it stops your health from regenerating until you buy an Elixir. Use the ledges around the meadow to get out of the fire, and keep moving so that the acid lands behind you. Beating it lets you help the children in the cage, which is what the mother elf has been asking for.

Robot Snake

Deep in a cave, the Robot Snake waits in a burrow in the wall. Press E at the hole and its head comes out. Only the head can be hurt. When its mouth starts to glow it floods the floor with fire, so go up to the plank walkway before the flames arrive. It also fires mines at the spot where you are standing, and they explode if you step on them, so keep shifting position. Now and then it pulls its head back to rest, and then it comes out again.

Sambo Elf (secret)

This elf wrestler hides in a secret place below the Kingdom. He stops for a moment, flickering between poses as a warning, and then charges forward, and if he catches you he lifts you up and slams you into the ground for heavy damage. He can only grab what is in front of him, so jump over him or get behind him when you see the warning. He also leaps on top of you and throws big rocks. After a slam he needs a moment to get up, and he is vulnerable during that time.

The Forest

Giant Tree

It never moves, but it does not need to. Roots burst up under every player, on the ground or on a ledge, with a crack in the earth as a warning. Spiky apples fly in an arc from its mouth and scatter thorns where they land. Its biggest attack takes a long time to prepare, then roots burst from the entire floor. When you see that warning, get up onto a ledge and wait it out.

Gorilla Rock

The stone gorilla lives in a grotto high above the Forest, and it picks its attack according to where most of the team is standing. If you are close on the ground it roars and shoves you hard. If you are far away, spikes of stone shoot up under your feet. If you are up on the ledges, rocks fall from the ceiling. A team that splits up forces it to choose, and it sometimes stops to rest, leaving it open to attack.

Zombie Harvest (secret)

Press E at the big carrot on the Forest farm and zombie vegetables climb out of the soil. Three are active at a time and fifteen must be destroyed in total. There are no walls in this fight and running away does not help, because they dig under the ground and come up next to you. Fight them one group at a time and do not let yourself get surrounded.

The Snow Base

Alter Santa

The fight starts when everyone has taken a present from the tree. Santa then arrives by sleigh, and he has a robot face under the beard. Against players on the ground he rolls a giant snowball that grows as it crosses the arena and bounces back once, so climb a ledge to avoid it. Against players on the ledges he throws gift boxes whose fuses burn before they explode. His sleigh also flies over and drops more gifts, and a mark shows where each one will land.

Robo Bigfoot

This enormous machine stands at the left edge of its arena, and only its face can be hurt. The two top ledges on the right are at face height, which makes them the best place to shoot from. Watch for the long warning before its fist comes down, its giant snowball that explodes where it lands, and its icy spit that slows you down and leaves patches of ice behind.

Ice Hockey Player (secret)

The strangest fight in the game takes place on an ice rink in a secret room. He blocks anything you aim at him, and his health only drops when your shots hit the big goal against the right wall of the room. There is no room behind it to hide in, and he will come into the net after you. On the ground he slides over and hits you towards the goal, and being knocked into it counts as a goal against you, with extra damage. If you stand on a ledge he shoots freezing pucks instead. Aim past him and leave yourself room to dodge.

The Swamp

Giant Maw Flower

A huge flower grows in the middle of a mud lake, and only its head can be hurt, so the highest ledges are the place to shoot from. The catch is that it knows this. It shoots a long vine at players on ledges, with a line as a warning, and getting hit ties you up. It also spits three thorns, or five when angry, and it pulls back its head before biting whoever is on the highest ledge. Do not fall into the lake, because the mud will slow you down.

Swamp Mage

Twice your height and wearing a dark robe, the Swamp Mage strikes the ground with her staff, and a line of roots comes up one after another in your direction, so step off the line instead of running along it. She also raises the staff to fire a green sphere that slowly turns to follow you and explodes when it hits something. When you beat her she gives up the fight for good and becomes a peaceful neighbour. Bring her gifts and she may eventually move in on your farm.

Swamp Crab (secret)

The Swamp's secret boss is disguised as a rock, so a boulder that seems out of place is worth a closer look. It has the most health of any boss in the location, so bring a full team or a well-levelled hero, along with some patience.

The Underworld

Bullkan

A colossal volcano golem rises out of the ground and never moves from the spot, so its two floating stone hands do all the work. First a fist smashes the ground, then lava erupts from under every player, with a warning on the ground just before it does. Next a fist follows you from above with its shadow below it, stops for a moment and smashes straight down. Keep moving and step out of the shadow once the fist stops. Finally a hand picks up a boulder and throws it at you, and it shatters where it lands. Only its head can be hurt, and shots that hit the body pass straight through. From the ground your attacks fly under its chin, so fight from the three ledges in front of it. The middle ledge is right in front of the head and is the best place for melee heroes. A ledge will not protect you from the hand or the lava.

Mr. Hol (secret)

Deep in a hidden room of the Underworld lives Mr. Hol, a red and black mole whose mining helmet covers his eyes. He fires stones from the cannon in his paws, three at a time. Then he dives into the ground: warning signs appear under your feet one after another, and five sharp drills shoot straight up out of the floor, high enough to reach the ledges. If you stand on the ground, he tunnels to you instead and bursts out right under your feet, throwing you into the air. Move as soon as you see a warning sign. While he is underground nothing can hurt him, so attack when he is up.

Fiery Hooves

A burning stag with antlers of fire runs across a wide stone floor with three ledges above it. If you stand on the ground, it stamps its hooves twice, and each stamp sends a wave of fire along the floor that you can jump over. Then it lowers its antlers and charges across the whole arena at great speed, so get up onto a ledge before it reaches you. If you are on a ledge, it throws five burning antlers at you, one after another. Never stay right behind it: it turns around and kicks with its hind legs, throwing you far away. It is open to attack while it catches its breath after a charge.

Skyland

Mr. Who

Mr. Who is a faceless blue hologram in a hat, and he holds a remote control. His arena has no ground at all, only 15 floating TV sets that you jump between. You cannot hurt Mr. Who himself, because your attacks pass straight through the hologram.

Look for the TV that shows a heart. That heart is his life, so hit that TV to damage him. The heart jumps to a different TV every 7 seconds, so keep moving.

When he presses the remote, the TV you are standing on shows a picture. A fire picture means flames will burst out of the top of that TV and burn you, so jump away. A hand picture means a fist will punch up out of the TV one second later. Two other TVs show a crosshair and fire arrows at you every 4 seconds, wherever you go.

T-Rex1000

T-Rex1000 is a giant robot dinosaur with no eyes and no nose, only a huge mouth full of metal teeth and a row of red spikes along its back. You reach its arena by climbing the floating steps above the last island of Skyland. The arena has a wide floor and four platforms above it.

Do not stand close to it. If you are near it on the ground, it turns its back to you and swings its heavy tail, which throws you far away. If you are on the ground but far from it, it walks to the middle of the arena and breathes fire to one side. The flame starts low along the floor and slowly rises until it points straight up, so run past it to the other side, behind its back, where the fire cannot reach. If you are up on a platform, it shoots sharp spikes from its back at you, three at a time.

Secret boss: Black Knight

High above the Skyland capital, a stairway of floating steps leads to a lonely stone island. It looks empty, but part of its floor is fake, and below it is a hidden hall that looks like a giant chessboard. Two huge black chess pieces stand there: a Queen and a King wearing a black crown. A checkpoint stands on the island's roof, so if you fall you come back right next to the hall.

Press E next to the King and reach for his crown. The Queen comes to life, draws a huge sword from her back and becomes the Black Knight. He charges across the board and knocks you away, swings his sword when you are close, and if you climb onto a platform he slashes the air and sends a phantom wave flying at you.

General advice

Before a hard fight, touch the nearest checkpoint and buy any cures you need. Spend the first minute just watching and dodging, because knowing a boss's pattern is worth more than a few early hits. And if a boss is still too strong, go back and level up: every level reduces the damage you take in The World.
