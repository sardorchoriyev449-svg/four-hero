Beginner's guide

4 Heroes is a side-scrolling action game for one to four players that runs in your browser. This guide covers what you need to know in your first hour, from your first room to your first boss.

Your first game

After you register, press Play and create a room. A room is your own copy of the game world, and up to three friends can join it with the room code. You can also play alone: the game does not wait for a full team, and bosses get weaker or stronger depending on how many players are fighting them.

Rooms open in The World, a huge map of floating islands. You begin in the Elf Kingdom next to the castle, the safest place in the game. Take a minute to walk through the village, talk to the elves and look at the shop before you jump to the next island.

Stamina decides most fights

Every attack and ability costs stamina, and it refills while you are not spending it. New players usually lose fights because they hold the attack button until the bar is empty and then have nothing left when a boss swings. Attack in short bursts and keep some stamina for your Shift ability, which is often the thing that saves you. The Knight spends stamina slowly on sword swings, while the Mage drains it quickly, so the same habit feels very different on each hero.

Levels make you tougher, not only stronger

Each hero has a separate level, so switching to a new hero means starting over with that hero. Defeating enemies, beating bosses and clearing maps all give experience. A new level can unlock a weapon, a skill or a bonus to damage and stamina: the heroes guide lists all of them.

In The World, your level also reduces the damage you take. At level 10 enemies deal about 80% of their normal damage to you, at level 20 about 67%, and at level 40 about half. That is why each location shows a recommended level on its banner when you enter it. If a location feels impossible, you are probably not doing anything wrong: go back and level up where the enemies are weaker.

How to earn coins

  • Fight: enemies and especially bosses pay coins. Crates and chests hidden around the islands hold more.
  • Daily rewards: signing in on consecutive days builds a seven-day reward chain, and three daily tasks pay extra.
  • Quests: villagers with a request will give you coins and experience when you finish it.
  • Farm: My Farm keeps earning while you are away, because crops grow when you are offline.

What to buy first

Open My Character to spend coins on your hero. Extra health is the safest first choice, because each level adds 20 maximum HP and gives you room for mistakes. Damage and stamina upgrades pay off once you are comfortable with the boss patterns. Skins and costumes in the Wardrobe are purely cosmetic, so leave them until your hero can survive.

Keep 50 coins in reserve. The Fat Elf's acid stops your HP from regenerating and electric attacks slow your stamina, and these effects stay until you buy a cure: an Elixir or a Chip, both sold in shops.

Five habits that help

  1. Touch every checkpoint flag. It is where you come back after you die.
  2. Read the warning before an attack. Almost every big boss attack has one: a crack in the ground, a glowing mouth or a mark on the floor.
  3. Fight near ledges. Many of the most dangerous attacks only cover the ground.
  4. Spread out in a team. Bosses choose attacks based on where most players are standing, so a split team is harder to hit.
  5. Look for Dragonet. This wild little dragon can appear on any map. Sneak up slowly and feed it ten times, and it becomes a companion that flies and fights beside you.
